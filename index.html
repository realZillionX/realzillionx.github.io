Hongji Chen (@ZillionX)

I am an undergraduate student at Fudan University (Class of 2029). I have been a research assistant at Fudan NLP Lab since October 2025. I am currently in NLP-LI Lab, advised by Assoc. Prof. Tao Gui. I am also a research intern at Shanghai AI Lab (October 2026 – present).

I received a Ph.D. offer from the Shanghai Innovation Institute in Summer 2026, becoming the youngest SII Ph.D. offer recipient up to that time.

My research focuses on CUA (Computer-Use Agent), RUA (Robot-Use Agent), and Agentic RL. I am dedicated to building a Super Agent spanning the Digital World and the Physical World, capable of real-world STEM experimentation and discovery to truly advance science and technology. Please feel free to contact me if you are interested in collaboration.

Sage S2.1 Flash
Contributor, Shanghai AI Lab

Deeply involved in improving the CUA capabilities of Sage S2.1 Flash, a 320B-parameter general-purpose MoE multimodal agent model. These capabilities support visual grounding, interface state tracking, and multi-step closed-loop execution across desktop, browser, and professional software workflows, achieving 85.2 on OSWorld-Verified and 67.6 on WebArena-Verified.
